📖 Metric Definitions
What every number means, how it is calculated, and what good looks like.
💵 Total Revenue Booked
"How much did we book?"
Sum of all agreed rates on confirmed loads. The direct financial output of the AI agent.
Σ agreed_rate where outcome = booked
↑ Higher is always better
⚡ Revenue Per Mile ($/mi)
"How efficient is the revenue we generate?"
Average dollar earned per mile across booked loads. A short high-rate load may be worth less than a long medium-rate one. This is the real profitability metric.
avg agreed_rate ÷ avg miles on booked loads
↑ Higher is better
📈 Booking Rate
"How many calls turn into business?"
Overall conversion across all calls. Includes rejected and unmatched calls so it will always be lower than the close rate — that is expected.
booked ÷ total calls × 100
Target: 40–60%
↑ Higher is better
🎯 Close Rate When Negotiating
"When we get to pricing — does the AI close?"
Only counts calls that reached price discussion with a verified carrier. Removes noise from rejections and unmatched loads.
booked ÷ (booked + no deal)
Target: 60%+
↑ Higher is better
😊 Carrier Satisfaction
"Are verified carriers happy with the experience?"
Only counts carriers who passed FMCSA verification — they are the ones who had a full conversation. Rejected carriers are excluded as they never reached a real interaction.
positive sentiment ÷ eligible calls
Target: 70%+
↑ Higher is better
✅ Carrier Verification Rate
"Are the carriers calling us legitimate?"
How many callers passed the FMCSA government authority check. A low rate is a lead quality problem — not an AI problem.
eligible ÷ total calls
↑ Higher is better
📦 Load Match Rate
"When a valid carrier calls, can we help them?"
Of verified carriers, how many were matched to an available load. A low rate means inventory gaps — ops needs to add loads in the lanes being requested.
matched ÷ eligible × 100
↑ Higher is better
⚖️ Avg Load Weight
"How heavy are the loads we're booking?"
Average weight in pounds of booked loads. Heavier loads typically command higher rates. If rates look low relative to weight, the negotiation ceiling may need adjusting.
sum of weight ÷ booked loads
🔄 Avg Counter-Offers
"How hard do carriers push back on price?"
Average number of times carriers asked for a different rate before the call ended. Lower means faster closes. Max allowed is 3 per call.
total counter-offer rounds ÷ negotiated calls
Target: below 1.5
↓ Lower is better
💹 Rate Saved vs Market
"Did we protect our margin?"
Total dollar difference between listed rate and what we agreed to pay. Positive = saved money. Negative = overpaid.
Σ (listed − agreed) on booked loads
Target: $0 or positive
↑ Positive (saved) is better
🗺️ Top Booked Lanes
"Where is our business coming from?"
Most frequently booked origin → destination pairs. The $/mi column shows which lanes are most profitable — use this to prioritise inventory.
count per lane + avg agreed_rate ÷ miles
↑ More bookings + higher $/mi = best lanes
📦 Commodity Breakdown
"What types of goods are we moving?"
Distribution of booked loads by commodity type. High concentration in one commodity = exposure if that market shifts.
count of booked loads per commodity_type
↑ Diversification across commodities is healthier

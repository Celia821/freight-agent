Metric Definitions — What Each Number Means
💵 Total Revenue Booked
"How much did we book?"
Sum of all agreed rates on confirmed loads. The direct financial output of the AI agent for this period.
Σ agreed_rate where outcome = booked
↑ Higher is always better
✅ Verification Pass Rate
"Are the carriers calling us legitimate?"
Measures lead quality from your carrier sources. A low rate means unqualified carriers are flooding the line.
eligible ÷ total calls × 100
↑ Higher is better
📦 Load Match Rate
"When a valid carrier calls, can we help them?"
Measures inventory coverage. Low rate = ops needs to add more loads in the lanes and equipment types being requested.
matched ÷ eligible × 100
↑ Higher is better
📈 Booking Rate
"How many calls turn into actual business?"
Overall conversion across all calls. Will naturally be lower than close rate because it includes rejected and unmatched calls.
booked ÷ total calls × 100
Industry target: 40–60%
↑ Higher is better
🎯 Close Rate When Negotiating
"When we get to pricing — does the AI close?"
The purest measure of AI negotiation quality. Only counts calls that actually reached price discussion.
booked ÷ (booked + no deal) × 100
Target: 60%+
↑ Higher is better
📊 Margin Protection
"Did we pay more or less than market price?"
Negative = paid below listed price = better margin for Acme. Positive = gave ground during negotiation to close the deal.
(agreed − listed price) ÷ listed price × 100
Target: 0% or negative
↓ Negative is better

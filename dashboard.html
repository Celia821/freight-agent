📖 Metric Definitions
What every number on this dashboard means, how it is calculated, and what good looks like.
💵 Total Revenue Booked
"How much did we book?"
Sum of all agreed rates on confirmed loads. The direct financial output of the AI agent for this period.
Σ agreed_rate where outcome = booked
↑ Higher is always better
⚡ Revenue Per Mile
"How efficient is the revenue we're generating?"
The average dollar earned per mile across booked loads. The real profitability metric — a short high-rate load can be worth less than a long medium-rate one.
avg agreed_rate ÷ avg miles on booked loads
Higher = more profitable loads being booked
↑ Higher is better
📈 Booking Rate
"How many calls turn into business?"
Overall conversion across all calls. Includes rejected and unmatched calls so it will always be lower than the close rate — that is normal.
booked ÷ total calls × 100
Industry target: 40–60%
↑ Higher is better
🎯 Close Rate When Negotiating
"When we get to pricing — does the AI close?"
Only counts calls that reached price discussion. The purest measure of AI negotiation quality — no noise from rejections or unmatched loads.
booked ÷ (booked + no deal)
Target: 60%+
↑ Higher is better
😊 Carrier Satisfaction
"Are carriers happy with the experience?"
Carriers who had a positive experience based on tone and language. Happy carriers call back more — directly drives future volume.
positive sentiment ÷ total calls × 100
Target: 70%+
↑ Higher is better
✅ Carrier Verification Rate
"Are the carriers calling us legitimate?"
How many callers passed the FMCSA authority check. A low rate is a lead quality problem — not an AI problem.
eligible ÷ total calls × 100
↑ Higher is better
📦 Load Match Rate
"When a valid carrier calls, can we help them?"
Of verified carriers, how many were matched to an available load. A low rate means inventory gaps — ops needs to add more loads in the lanes being requested.
matched ÷ eligible × 100
↑ Higher is better
⚖️ Avg Load Weight
"How heavy are the loads we're booking?"
Average weight in pounds of booked loads. Heavier loads typically command higher rates. If rates look low relative to weight, the negotiation ceiling may need adjusting.
sum of weight ÷ booked loads
↑ Heavier loads should mean higher rates
🔄 Avg Counter-Offers
"How hard do carriers push back on price?"
Average number of times carriers asked for a different rate. Lower means faster closes. Max allowed is 3 per call.
total counter-offer rounds ÷ negotiated calls
Target: below 1.5
↓ Lower is better
💹 Rate Saved vs Market
"Did we protect our margin?"
Total dollar difference between the listed rate and what we agreed to pay. Positive = saved money vs market. Negative = paid above market.
Σ (listed − agreed) on booked loads
Target: $0 or positive
↑ Positive (saved) is better
🗺️ Top Booked Lanes
"Where is our business coming from?"
Most frequently booked origin → destination pairs. High-volume lanes should always have loads available. The $/mile column shows which lanes are most profitable.
count of booked calls per lane + avg $/mile per lane
↑ More bookings + higher $/mile = best lanes
📦 Commodity Breakdown
"What types of goods are we moving?"
Distribution of booked loads by commodity type. Shows concentration risk — if one commodity dominates, the brokerage is exposed if that market shifts.
count of booked loads per commodity_type
↑ Diversification across commodities is healthier

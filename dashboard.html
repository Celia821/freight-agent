KPI Reference — How Each Metric Is Calculated
💵 Total Revenue Booked
Sum of all agreed rates on successfully booked loads. The primary business output of the AI agent.
Σ agreed_rate where outcome = booked
↑ Higher is always better
📈 Booking Rate
Overall conversion rate. Includes all call types — ineligible carriers lower this rate even though they were correctly rejected.
booked ÷ total calls × 100
Industry target: 40–60%
↑ Higher is better
🎯 Negotiation Win Rate
Closes the loop on agent persuasiveness. Only counts calls that actually reached negotiation — removes ineligible and no-match noise.
booked ÷ (booked + no_deal) × 100
Target: 60%+
↑ Higher is better
🚫 Ineligible Rate
Carriers rejected by FMCSA verification. High values are a lead quality problem — not an AI problem. Ops should review inbound carrier sources.
ineligible ÷ total calls × 100
Target: <15%
↓ Lower is better
📦 No Load Match Rate
Eligible carriers turned away because no load matched their equipment or lane. Signals inventory gaps — ops should add more loads in those corridors.
no_load_match ÷ eligible calls × 100
Target: <20%
↓ Lower is better
📊 Rate vs Loadboard
Measures margin protection. Negative means we paid less than listed — better for the brokerage. Positive means we overpaid to close the deal.
(agreed - loadboard) ÷ loadboard × 100
Target: ≤0% (pay at or below board)
↓ Lower or negative is better
